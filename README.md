This application is an AI-POWERED OPERATIONS AND DECISION-SUPPORT PLATFORM for automotive CASTING AND MACHINING / AUTO ANCILLARY UNITS, 
esigned to improve MANUFACTURING QUALITY, OPERATIONAL EFFICIENCY, EQUIPMENT RELIABILITY, AND PROFITABILITY. 
It integrates COMPUTER VISION FOR CASTING DEFECT DETECTION, DIMENSIONAL AND STRUCTURAL INSPECTION, PREDICTIVE MAINTENANCE, TOOL-WEAR PREDICTION, 
AI-BASED DEMAND AND INVENTORY FORECASTING, ENERGY OPTIMIZATION, and AI-ASSISTED TRACEABILITY AND ROOT-CAUSE ANALYSIS to transform shop-floor data 
into actionable intelligence. The platform can use SYNTHETIC OR REAL CSV/EXCEL DATA, generate KPIs, ANALYTICS, VISUALIZATIONS, METHODOLOGIES, AND EXPORTABLE
REPORTS, and support data-driven manufacturing decisions. Its key benefits include REDUCED SCRAP AND REWORK, EARLIER EQUIPMENT-FAILURE DETECTION, OPTIMIZED TOOL 
UTILIZATION, LOWER INVENTORY AND ENERGY COSTS, FASTER ROOT-CAUSE INVESTIGATION, IMPROVED PRODUCT QUALITY, and BETTER PRODUCTION PLANNING, with the application 
illustrating potential improvements such as 25% LOWER UNPLANNED DOWNTIME, 15–25% LOWER INVENTORY, 8–20% LOWER ENERGY COSTS, and OVER 99% FASTER 
ROOT-CAUSE ANALYSIS.
